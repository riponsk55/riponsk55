About Me

I am a technical professional with hands-on experience in the Telecommunication Technical Department, Telecom Power/Infrastructure Support, and the Telecommunication IT Sector.

My field expertise includes NTTN, ISP & network support, fiber optic splicing, MikroTik configuration, and end-to-end connectivity from router to OLT to ONU. I also work with Radius Manager, customer billing, and technical troubleshooting.

Beyond telecom, I am passionate about web development — creating responsive websites, WordPress & Blogspot customizations, eCommerce solutions, and landing pages using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Tailwind CSS. I run Roja Shop (an online store for Fruits, Honey & Dried Fish), manage DocBD.org (a doctor listing platform), and create tech content on Facebook & YouTube. I integrate Google Forms, Telegram Bots, and WhatsApp to build smart digital experiences.
